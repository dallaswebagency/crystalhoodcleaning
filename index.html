A clean exhaust keeps your kitchen running

Our commercial kitchen hood cleaning services keep your kitchen safe, compliant and functioning at its best. We understand how important it is to maintain your kitchen’s exhaust system.

Prevent fire hazards

Grease builds up inside hoods, ducts and fans. Regular degreasing removes it before it becomes a risk.

Improve air quality

A clean exhaust system moves smoke and heat out properly, so your kitchen stays comfortable to work in.

Your kitchen exhaust system, cooking line to rooftop fan.

    Kitchen exhaust system cleaning and degreasing

    We clean and degrease the hood, filters and ductwork so grease doesn’t build up where you can’t see it.

  • Ductwork

    Access panels open up the duct run so every section of it can be reached and cleaned properly.
